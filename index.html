Kural Reader — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 3 October 2026 · Kural Corp

Kural Reader is a prayer reader. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics, no crash reporting and no tracking. It does not connect to the internet: the Android release build does not even request the internet permission.

What stays on your device

To remember how you like to read, the app stores these on your device, in its own private storage:

  • your Favorites and the order you put them in;
  • your preferences — script/language, font, verse and meaning sizes, theme, background, and whether meanings and verse numbers are shown;
  • the verse you last reached in each prayer, so the Reader reopens where you left off.

None of this is sent to us or anyone else. Uninstalling the app deletes it. If you have turned on your phone's own backup (Google backup on Android, iCloud on iPhone), your phone may include it in that backup; that backup is run by Google or Apple under your account, and we cannot see it.

The Feedback form — the only thing you can send us

Feedback is optional. The form asks for:

FieldRequiredSent?
NameYesYes, as you typed it
EmailNoYes if you gave one; otherwise the message says “(not given)”
Comment (up to 2,000 characters)YesYes, as you typed it
The small sum (“3 + 4 = ?”)YesNo. It only checks a person is using the form.

When you tap Submit, the app does not send anything itself. It opens your own email app with a message already filled in:

  • To: the Kural Corp feedback address, vijaya@duck.com
  • Subject: Kural Reader feedback
  • Body: Name: …, Email: …, a blank line, then your comment.

That is the whole message. The app adds no device identifier, model, operating-system version, app version, location, language or time. You can read, change or discard the message in your email app; nothing is sent unless you send it there. Because it is an ordinary email from your own account, we will also receive your sending email address and the usual email headers your provider adds.

We use feedback only to read and reply to it and to improve the app. We keep it in our mailbox for as long as it is useful for that, and delete it on request. We never sell or share it.

Share App

Share App opens your device's standard share sheet with a short piece of text about the app. You choose where it goes; the app is not told what you chose or who you sent it to.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and collects no personal information from anyone, children included. Feedback is the only way to send us anything, and it only goes if the person sends the email themselves.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date. If the app ever starts sending anything over the internet, this policy — and the store privacy labels — will be updated before that version is released.

Contact

Questions about privacy: use the Feedback form in the app, or email vijaya@duck.com.
